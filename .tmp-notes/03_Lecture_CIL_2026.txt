Stochastic gradient descent
SGD picks a data point at random (↙ϱ or uniformly from the
sample) and evolves the iterates
W ∈W ≃ς∞W ϖ(x; VW),
V ∈V ≃ς∞V ϖ(x; VW)
where ς > 0 is a step size (or learning rate).

↭However, non-linear autoencoders are often trained with
gradient descent methods without global convergence
guarantees. This may lead to worse non-linear autoencoders
than linear ones.
