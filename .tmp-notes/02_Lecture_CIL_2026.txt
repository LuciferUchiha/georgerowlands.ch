Let V be a vector space and U a subspace of V .
A linear transformation P : V →V is called a projection onto U
if, for every x ⇒V :
1. Projection Property: P(x) ⇒U, meaning P(x) is an

2. Idempotent Property: P(P(x)) = P(x) for all x ⇒V .
