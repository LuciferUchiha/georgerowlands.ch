1. For A ↑Rn↑m, ﬁnd a semi-orthogonal Q ↑Rn↑2k with
few(er) columns s.t. A →QQ→A.
2. Perform SVD of the smaller matrix B := Q→A = ˜U˜# ˜V→
3. Extend it to an approximate SVD for A →(Q ˜U)˜# ˜V→.
4. In computing the SVD of B, we can apply the same technique
to its rows, thereby reducing both columns and rows of A.
