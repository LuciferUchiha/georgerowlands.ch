Refresher: spectral decomposition
Any symmetric matrix A can be expressed as:
A = VDV T
where:
↭V = [v1, v2, . . . , vn] is the matrix whose columns are the
orthonormal eigenvectors of A.
↭D = diag(ω1, ω2, . . . , ωn) is the diagonal matrix whose
diagonal elements are the eigenvalues of A.
↭V T is the transpose of V .

Low-rank approximation using SVD
Any matrix A of size n ↓k can be approximated by:
A ↘U!V T,
where:
↭U is an orthonormal matrix of size n ↓r
↭! is a diagonal matrix of size r ↓r
↭V is an orthonormal matrix of size r ↓k
where r ↗k is the rank of the desired approximation.
