Non-Negative Matrix Factorization (NMF)
NMF is a standalone model used to approximate non-negative data
matrices.
Why non-negativity?
↭Many real-world data types are naturally non-negative (e.g.,
counts, intensities, frequencies).
↭Negative values in factorization often have no physical
meaning in such cases.
Key motivation: Ensure non-negative approximations by design.
This is achieved by constraining matrix factors to be
non-negative – a built-in inductive bias in the model.
