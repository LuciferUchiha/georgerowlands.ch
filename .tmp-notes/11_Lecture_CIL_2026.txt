VAE: Take Home Message
What VAEs give us
⇒Principled likelihood-based training via the ELBO
⇒A structured latent space: smooth interpolation,
arithmetic
⇒Latent dimensions can capture interpretable factors
(rotation, smile, hair)
What’s still open
⇒Samples are often blurry; Gaussian decoder averages
over modes
⇒Disentanglement is not guaranteed; needs ϱ-VAE or
stronger priors
⇒ELBO is only a lower bound; gap between bound
and true likelihood
Extensions: ε-VAE (disentanglement), VQ-VAE (discrete latents),
hierarchical VAEs, normalizing-ﬂow priors.

Lecture Summary
Autoregressive models – factor p(x;ω) exactly via the
chain rule
⇒DNN parameterizes each p(xt | x<t;ω)
⇒PixelCNN, GPT – masked convolution / self-attention
⇒Training: parallel, exact MLE. Generation: sequential.
Variational autoencoders – latent z, optimize the ELBO
⇒Encoder qε(z|x) approximates intractable pω(z|x)
⇒Reparameterization: gradients through sampling
⇒Structured latent: interpolation, factors of variation
Trade-o”: AR – exact likelihood, no latent. VAE – structured latent,
only a lower bound.
